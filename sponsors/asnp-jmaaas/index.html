ASNP Applies For Every Job So You Don’t Have To

30 July 2026 · 7 min

Do you find that the job market is motherfucking, cunting, twatting, bitching, whoring shit-fucked?

Did you assume that if employment became this degrading, opaque, repetitive and irrational, it must at least be because jobs were no longer necessary?

Did you imagine we had finally automated enough of civilisation that people could become artists, philosophers, amateur historians, full-time hedonists, coastal drifters, or whatever else human beings might do if they weren’t still beholden to the necessity of selling their waking hours to survive?

Yeah.

Turns out jobs are still necessary to make money.

Money is still necessary to obtain food, shelter, healthcare, electricity, transport and the legal right to remain indoors.

The jobs are just harder to get now.

Introducing ASNP - Application Spray-n-Pray, the agentic AI platform that applies for every remotely plausible vacancy so you don’t have to spend your remaining life copying the same employment history into different broken forms.

ASNP is a JMAaaS (Job Mass-Apply-as-a-Service) platform built for candidates who have accepted that employment is now a spam problem.

Upload your CV.

Add your work history.

List your preferred roles, salary range, locations, industries, deal-breakers, technical skills, tolerance for hybrid-work lies, and willingness to pretend you have always dreamed of joining a medium-sized payments company in North Sydney.

Then give ASNP access to SEEK, Indeed, LinkedIn, company career portals and any other website where an organisation has placed a vacancy behind seventeen mandatory fields and a CAPTCHA.

ASNP monitors the market around the clock.

It identifies suitable roles. You do not have to read the Position Description. ASNP’s philosophy is “apply first, read the JD if you get a callback from a recruiter”.

Then it navigates the user interface. It fills out the application. It adapts your CV. It writes a tailored cover letter. It inserts one or two deliberate punctuation errors so the application looks human.

And it submits the whole thing before another desperate bastard can.

“Our users told us they were exhausted by the job search,” said founder Nathan Norton. “They wanted a more efficient way to be ignored at scale.”

ASNP does not rely on simple browser automation.

Its agentic AI can interpret job advertisements, compare requirements with your background, infer which criteria are probably fake, and determine whether a role described as “entry level” actually requires eight years of experience, three cloud certifications and prior ownership of a multinational payments platform.

When ASNP encounters a straightforward application, it completes it automatically.

When it encounters an unexpected question, broken portal, psychometric test, identity check, unpaid assessment, video response, salary trap, or request to explain why you are passionate about enterprise payroll software, it sends you an email requesting manual intervention. It is then trained on that question and your answer so it can answer future instances automatically.

ASNP is available in three tiers.

Basic Supplication#

For applicants who still possess a trace of dignity and want to destroy it gradually.

Includes:

Up to 50 applications per month.

One uploaded CV.

Basic job matching.

Automated cover letters.

Standard rejection tracking.

And a weekly report showing how many companies have failed to acknowledge your existence.

Professional Carpet Bombing#

For experienced candidates who understand that quality applications are important but deep down, it’s still a numbers game.

Includes:

Up to 500 applications per month.

Multiple CV variants.

Automatic salary-answer optimisation.

Recruiter-message handling.

Cross-platform application tracking.

Deliberate punctuation imperfection.

And Ghost Detection, which identifies roles repeatedly advertised for months despite apparently never being filled.

Professional users also receive Cover Letter Authenticity Mode, which adds phrases such as:

“I was excited to see this opportunity.”

“My background aligns strongly with the role.”

“I am particularly drawn to your organisation’s mission.”

And:

“I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience could contribute to your team.”

None of which you are required to feel.

Enterprise Desperation#

For contractors, migrants, graduates, redundant middle managers, burned-out technologists, and anyone whose financial runway has become a countdown timer.

Includes:

Unlimited applications.

Twenty-four-hour market monitoring.

Full multi-platform agentic browsing.

Automatic company-account creation.

Custom CV generation for every role.

Recruiter follow-up messages.

Application-status inference.

Interview-likelihood scoring.

And Shame-Free Reapplication, which submits you again when the same company reposts the same vacancy six weeks later with slightly different wording.

Enterprise Desperation also includes Reciprocal Automation, allowing ASNP to communicate directly with employer-side AI systems.

Your AI submits the application.

Their AI rejects it.

Your AI sends a polite follow-up.

Their AI thanks you for your interest.

No human being need ever become aware that the interaction occurred.

Early users have praised the platform for restoring a sense of control.

“Before ASNP, I spent four hours tailoring each application,” said one customer. “Now the system sends 80 applications while I stare at the wall and experience the same result much more efficiently.”

Another user said the software had dramatically increased his exposure to opportunity.

“I’ve applied for 1,400 jobs in seven weeks,” he said. “I’ve had two automated interviews, one recruiter asking whether I have Australian working rights despite being born here and can trace my ancestry to the Second Fleet, and a rejection email addressed to another candidate. The momentum is incredible.”

Future updates will include Screening-Call-as-a-Service, where a second agent attends initial screening calls on your behalf and answers questions using your uploaded accomplishments, preferred anecdotes and approved level of enthusiasm.

A later release will support Interview-Preparation-as-a-Service, where an agent will study your facial microexpressions and stammering as it asks questions tailored to your alleged background and provide feedback feedback loops on your performance, interview posture and vocal cadence.

It will also simulate alternative answers you could have given in parallel timelines where you were more confident, less honest, or born into a different labour market entirely.

ASNP does not fix the job market.

It does not explain the job market.

It does not ask why millions of people are competing for fewer credible roles while companies insist there is a talent shortage.

It does not investigate why hiring processes expanded from two conversations into a nine-stage ritual involving recruiters, panels, take-home assignments, psychometric tests, cultural interviews, reference checks, background checks, AI video analysis and a final rejection sent from an unmonitored inbox.

It simply gives you a larger shovel.

Because if the system has turned employment into spam, you may as well become better at spam.

ASNP. They automated the rejection. We automated the grovelling.

ASNP does not investigate the root causes of a job market that is motherfucking, cunting, twatting, bitching, whoring shit-fucked, nor does it identify the governments, central banks, corporations, consultants, outsourcing firms, technology platforms, mass-migration settings, lockdown decisions, monetary interventions, hiring managers, recruiters, executives or assorted institutional cowards responsible.

Although, let’s face it, mate, a great deal of the current arrangement can be traced back to early 2020, when a microscopic export from a large Asian country helped persuade governments to suspend normal life, detonate labour markets, print oceans of money, scramble supply chains, distort housing, normalise emergency management and then it retreated to its gated communities while letting the rest of you fend for yourselves.

ASNP treats symptoms only. It does not guarantee employment, interviews, accurate job matching, truthful job advertisements, competent recruiters, disclosed salary ranges, functioning application portals, human acknowledgement, meaningful work, affordable housing, dignity, solvency or escape. Automated applications may contain errors, fabricated enthusiasm, awkward wording, inappropriate claims, accidental honesty, incorrect company names, references to technologies you used once in 2018, or the phrase “I am excited to apply” in circumstances where no excitement exists. Excessive use may result in duplicate applications, blacklisting, recruiter irritation, platform suspension, employer-side bot detection, or the discovery that the vacancy was never real. In the case of advert exhaustion—where all available suitable job advertisements have been applied to, re-applied to, or algorithmically hallucinated into submission—no refunds will be issued. All sales are final, including situations where there is literally nothing left to apply for.
