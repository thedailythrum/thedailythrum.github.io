CVjuvenate Makes Your Career Look Twenty Years Younger

28 October 2026 · 4 min

Are you an experienced office worker?

Have you spent twenty years developing expertise, solving problems, delivering projects and learning how organisations actually function?

Do you remember when employers valued experience?

Well, congratulations.

You’re now a liability.

Introducing CVjuvenate™, the revolutionary CV anti-ageing treatment that smooths away the visible signs of a long and productive career.

Because employers want experience.

Just not that much experience.

Turn back the career clock#

CVjuvenate uses advanced AI-powered cosmetic résumé technology to identify and treat the seven visible signs of professional ageing.

Career Wrinkles

Twenty-five years of employment can leave deep chronological lines across your CV.

CVjuvenate smooths them away by removing positions older than ten years, reducing the appearance of career maturity without disturbing your core competencies.

Experience Sagging

Does your employment history extend onto a third page?

Our proprietary CareerLift™ treatment tightens your professional profile, lifting excess experience away from the recruiter’s field of vision.

Seniority Lines

Have you previously held titles such as Principal Engineer, Head of Architecture, Programme Director or General Manager?

CVjuvenate softens these intimidating features into younger-looking alternatives.

“Senior Technical Professional.”

“Delivery Specialist.”

“Hands-On Contributor.”

You still have the expertise.

You simply no longer look expensive.

Chronological Pigmentation

CVjuvenate detects and removes age-revealing blemishes including:

  • Graduation dates before 2010.
  • References to Windows NT, Visual Basic 6 and NET 3.0 or lower.
  • Twenty-year service awards.
  • Early-career experience involving fax machines.
  • Certifications issued by organisations that no longer exist.
  • The phrase “over 25 years of experience”.

Replace “25 years of experience” with the youthful, radiant:

“Extensive hands-on experience.”

Same person.

Same skills.

Less evidence.

Clinically tested on recruiters#

In our laboratory trials, recruiters were shown two CVs belonging to the same candidate.

The untreated CV described a 49-year-old software engineer with 26 years of experience, extensive architectural knowledge and a history of successful delivery.

The CVjuvenate-treated version described a “dynamic, hands-on technology professional with extensive experience delivering modern cloud-native solutions”.

Recruiters described the treated candidate as:

“Agile.”

“Adaptable.”

“Probably a good cultural fit.”

“Someone we could develop.”

The untreated candidate was described as:

“Potentially overqualified.”

“May struggle with change.”

“Could be set in their ways.”

“Likely to have salary expectations.”

Both CVs belonged to the same fucking person.

“Our research found that experienced candidates were being penalised for having visibly experienced things,” said CVjuvenate founder Nathan Norton.

“We asked ourselves: what if we could preserve the competence while removing the evidence?”

Introducing our Advanced Anti-Ageing Formula#

CVjuvenate Premium includes Salary Expectation Concealer™, which removes indicators that you may remember what a competitive salary looked like.

Legacy Technology Exfoliation™ removes references to systems that employers are still running but would rather pretend they aren’t.

Leadership Line Relaxer™ softens previous management experience so hiring managers don’t become uncomfortable about interviewing somebody who may have done their job before.

And Youthful Enthusiasm Injection™ adds phrases such as:

“Passionate about continuous learning.”

“Excited to contribute.”

“Thrives in fast-paced environments.”

“Always eager to embrace emerging technologies.”

Because nothing says seasoned professional like pretending you’re thrilled to discover another JavaScript framework.

Premium users can additionally activate Career Age Ambiguity™, which removes graduation years, early employment dates and other indicators that you may have been professionally active before the interviewer was born.

The CVjuvenate Promise#

We cannot make employers less prejudiced.

We cannot restore the labour market you entered twenty-five years ago.

We cannot guarantee that the recruiter will recognise the value of somebody who has already solved the problems their organisation is about to encounter.

But we can make your CV look as though you haven’t been around long enough to know better.

CVjuvenate™.

You’ve earned your experience. Now hide the evidence.

CVjuvenate™ does not prevent the interviewer from noticing that you are, in fact, the age you are. While CVjuvenate™ may successfully conceal twenty-five years of professional experience on paper, results may deteriorate significantly when you attend an interview and expose your actual face to another human being. Video interviews, in-person interviews and unexpected requests to turn your camera on may result in immediate chronological disclosure.

CVjuvenate™ cannot guarantee employment, prevent age discrimination, eliminate concerns about being overqualified or stop a hiring manager fifteen years your junior from explaining modern software development to you. Even if successfully hired, your salary remains subject to prevailing market ceilings, employer budgets, internal pay bands and the economic reality that twenty-five years of accumulated expertise does not entitle you to twenty-five years of accumulated bargaining power. Your employer may expect you to bring the judgement of a seasoned professional while accepting the remuneration of somebody who discovered Git last Thursday. Continued use constitutes acceptance that you have spent your entire working life becoming more experienced, only to discover that the labour market would prefer you to be younger, cheaper and somehow equally competent.
