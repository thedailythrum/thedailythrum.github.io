Report: Everything Is Performative, Fake, Subversive And Our Reality Is Curated By Powerful Factions

30 July 2026 · 5 min

A preliminary review of modern history has concluded that nothing happened, nobody meant anything, all major institutions are fronts, and the public has been living inside a heavily curated simulation experiment since at least 1973.

The reasons for this remain unclear, although the report alleges that several powerful factions are simply sadists and that the Western world now functions as a planet-sized terrarium from which they derive pleasure by watching the population become frightened, indebted, medicated, suspicious and controlled.

Researchers who compiled the report requested anonymity.

They found that politics, sport, war, medicine, finance and celebrity culture consist largely of pre-decided “wag-the-dog” narratives designed to maintain emotional agitation while preventing the public from noticing that nobody in charge appears to believe in anything.

Among the report’s preliminary findings:

  • Football results have been generated in advance since 1986 by a Zurich spreadsheet called SPORTS_OUTCOMES_FINAL_v7_REAL.xlsx. Players are informed only moments before kickoff which side has been selected to lose.
  • Elections remain technically real, but votes are counted solely to generate localised graphics. National outcomes are selected in advance according to donor comfort, market confidence and whether the public requires a brief but convincing sensation of revenge.
  • Taliban founder Mullah Mohammed Omar was not a man but a role assigned to whichever one-eyed cleric was available. His death remained undisclosed for two years because production had not yet decided whether to recast him.
  • Malaysia Airlines Flight 370 never existed. The aircraft, passengers, grieving relatives, satellite data, search vessels and documentaries were created to determine how long the public would follow a story after being repeatedly told there was no new information.
  • Celebrity scandals are produced by a joint casting office in Los Angeles and Davos. Minor celebrities are paired with opinions, addictions, apologies and non-binary children according to quarterly engagement targets.
  • The global financial system contains no money. Figures displayed in banking applications are behavioural prompts intended to make citizens attend work and avoid speaking honestly during meetings.
  • Medical waiting rooms are not connected to treatment facilities. They are endurance chambers used to identify which citizens can be made to tolerate worsening pain while completing the same form three times.
  • Regional rail replacement buses are deployed even when no rail work is occurring. Their sole purpose is to introduce additional friction into otherwise manageable journeys.

Wuhan Virus#

The report also alleges that the Wuhan Virus never existed and that the pandemic was instead a coordinated operation with two principal objectives.

The first was to induce prolonged trauma, isolation and low-grade mental illness across the world, leaving billions of people permanently trapped in survival mode.

The second was to inject the global population with dormant nanites capable of killing billions at the push of a button once artificial intelligence becomes sufficiently competent to perform most jobs.

Researchers conceded that the operation would not require flawless cooperation among governments, pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, universities, journalists, regulators, logistics firms, family doctors and nurses.

“You can simply do things,” one researcher said. “There is a fix-everything button. It is only described as impossible when the proposed outcome would benefit you.”

Cost of living#

The report further concluded that the simultaneous cost-of-living crisis across multiple Western countries was not the result of inflation, supply shocks, energy prices, housing shortages or interest-rate policy. These are just words to most people. It is like telling them their suffering is weather: unfortunate, complicated and beyond anyone’s control.

Prices had been deliberately raised by a transnational elite to keep the population in a permanent state of low-level panic, forcing millions to spend every waking hour calculating rent, groceries, fuel, childcare and whether one unexpected dental bill make them cross the line to destitution.

Researchers rejected the assumption that the objective was profit.

“They already possess more money than their great-great-great-great-grandchildren could spend without making an organised effort,” the report said.

“To understand the motive, imagine a small child in a garden pulling the legs from insects.”

“He is not doing it for economic gain. He is doing it because he can.”

Elites#

The review further found that public disagreements between elites are largely scripted.

Politicians who appear to hate one another reportedly eat together after filming. Media commentators receive opposing positions from the same producer. Corporate whistleblowers are introduced only after the relevant executives have exercised their options. Protest movements are allocated police routes, approved slogans and a limited number of overturned bins to preserve the appearance of democratic friction.

“People assume competing institutions must have competing interests,” one researcher said.

“They do. They compete over who gets to operate the terrarium.”

The team also found that many public figures are composite characters portrayed by rotating actors.

Some politicians have reportedly been played by as many as six people, although viewers failed to notice because each version used the same phrases: “hard-working families”, “difficult decisions”, “lessons will be learned” and “now is not the time”.

Never let a good crisis go to waste. If you don’t have one, create one.#

The report’s most disturbing conclusion was not that reality had been fabricated, but that the fabrication had become increasingly lazy.

Early operations involved physical sets, trained actors, forged documents and plausible motivations.

Modern events are reportedly generated by language models using prompts such as:

Output the premise of a national crisis that curtails civil liberties, lowers trust and can be blamed on ordinary people.

The resulting material is then approved by consultants, translated into twelve languages and presented to the public beneath a graphic reading WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW.

“At first they wanted us to believe the story,” one researcher said.

“Now they merely want us to repeat it while knowing it is false.”

The report recommends no action, noting that all resistance movements were included in the original production schedule.

At publishing time, the researchers had disappeared following what authorities described as a routine single-vehicle accident involving two trains, a helicopter and a passenger aircraft.
